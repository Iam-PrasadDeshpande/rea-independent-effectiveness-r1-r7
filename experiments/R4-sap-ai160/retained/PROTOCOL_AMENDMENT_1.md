# Treatment adherence amendment

The first B session had the AST tool available but made zero AST invocations.
Its evidence and cost are retained as a pilot, not counted as an AST-use result.
A fresh replacement B session must use at least one successful AST operation.
C must similarly make a real REA invocation and use its result. A already used
its assigned standard inspection class. This amendment enforces the intended
tool treatments; it changes no application bytes, hidden cases, rubric,
objectives, model settings, 18-call budget or 15-minute per-session limit.

There are three primary arms plus one retained B pilot. This is a disclosed
protocol amendment after a tool-adoption failure, not a pristine preregistered
confirmatory trial. No peer or evaluator findings are passed to replacement B.
All costs of the pilot remain charged to this mission and are reported.
