# Limitations

Seven deliberately selected applications and single matched treatments do not support broad statistical generalization. Denominators and tool interfaces differ. High bounded agreement can have a ceiling effect. Architecture grades are not blinded independent human gold. Setup, ordering, caches, transport, provider lifecycles and recovery histories confound time/token comparisons; no monetary costs are invented.

R1 has no B arm and an unresolved original launcher freeze mismatch. R2's published package provenance and pinned source are distinct identities. R3's REA primary core analysis timed out. R4 reconstructs a timestamp helper, not the complete purchase-order agent or live SAP environment. R5 all-arm reminder failures remain failures. R6 is INVALID_COMPARISON irrespective of its retained counts. R7 uses original codec binaries, preserves raw C 29/30, and adds the verified classifier correction without rewriting history.

The original R6 final archive is unavailable. The new verified supplemental archive is owner-local and explicitly identified as new. Public payload coverage is partial because raw logs, vendor software, workstation details and other uncleared material are withheld; exact identities and verified local availability are recorded. No remote asset download is claimed for withheld evidence. See [evidence policy](EVIDENCE-INDEX.md).

Digital signatures bind a current candidate to an existing locally trusted identity. They do not certify publisher identity to outside trust stores, establish historical launcher continuity, prove behavioral correctness, or constitute owner acceptance of a historical exception. FINAL_FREEZE_HOLD remains in effect.
